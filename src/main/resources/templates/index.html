How Campus Find Works

Three straightforward steps to reconnect students with their missing belongings.

1

Report in Seconds

Submit a report for any item you've misplaced or discovered on campus. Specify the building, date, distinctive marks, and how to reach you.

2

Instant Search & Match

Filter by category, campus zone, or report type. Our real-time query engine searches descriptions to locate matching belongings immediately.

3

Claim & Recover Safely

Verify ownership with the finder or campus lost & found desk (using serial numbers or student ID). Mark the report as recovered with one click.
